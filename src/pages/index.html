Let's put the Pride colors back on 4th & Colorado.

The rainbow crosswalk at the heart of Austin's LGBTQ+ district came up in July under a state order. Here is the happy part: this block has been gaining color ever since. Neighbors painted a mural in June. The city dedicated an LGBTQIA+ historical marker on 21 August. One thing is still missing, and San Antonio has already shown a Texas city can do it lawfully, with no council vote: the sidewalk. Here is how to ask, in about five minutes.
