The short version, without JavaScript

In July 2026 the state required Austin to remove the rainbow crosswalk at West 4th and Colorado. San Antonio, under the identical order, painted its sidewalks instead: the order governs the roadway, and sidewalks belong to the city. Around $170,000, dedicated in March 2026, still there, and it needed no council vote because that corridor already held Pride Cultural Heritage District status.

Austin can do the same. The two asks are a rainbow sidewalk treatment at 4th and Colorado, and an LGBTQ+ cultural heritage district designation for the West 4th corridor.

To act without this page: email all eleven council members, call the Mayor on 512-978-2100 or District 9 on 512-978-2109, or sign up to speak at a council meeting.
